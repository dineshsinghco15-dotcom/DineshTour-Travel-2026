Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

At Dinesh Tour & Travel, we prioritize the confidentiality and protection of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, protected, and utilized when booking vacation packages or contacting our travel consultants.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We collect essential information required to process your tour reservations, including your full name, email address, contact phone number, travel dates, and passenger counts. Your personal information is strictly used for booking flight tickets, hotel rooms, luxury cab transfers, and tour permits.

2. Data Protection & Security

Your personal data is encrypted and securely stored. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Data is shared exclusively with certified travel partners (airlines, hotels, local guides) necessary to execute your booked itinerary.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations made 15+ days prior to departure qualify for a 100% full refund. Partial refunds (50%) apply for cancellations between 7 to 14 days before departure date. Rescheduling is allowed free of cost up to 7 days prior to travel.

4. Contact & Support

For privacy concerns or inquiries regarding your stored booking records, please reach out to our privacy officer at privacy@dineshtravel.com.
